Before applying

Connect both adapters. For a new arrangement, disconnect VPNs during initial setup. Configure the router’s WAN to receive an address automatically (DHCP).

Relay only connects or disconnects your VPN. Create or select a working configuration in the VPN app first; Relay does not edit its servers, accounts or profiles.

VPN control and permissions

Relay detects VPN connections registered in macOS whenever Settings opens and uses macOS to request connection changes. Availability does not guarantee a working server or a shareable full tunnel. OpenVPN and Happ have separate app controls when no system connection is registered.

Relay uses macOS to read VPN connection status and request connection changes. macOS may ask you to approve VPN permissions. Administrator authorization is requested when installing the sharing helper; it is not needed for each status check.

Connect On Demand

Connect On Demand automatically starts or reconnects a VPN when its network rules match. It can reconnect immediately after Relay requests a disconnect. Disable this option yourself in your VPN app or its macOS VPN settings before using Relay to control it.

For V2Box Pro: Settings → Advanced Settings → On Demand Settings → turn On Demand off. To keep automatic VPN connection on the Mac, enable Keep Mac VPN unchanged in Relay instead.
