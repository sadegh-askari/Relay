Internet sharing, one button.

Relay 1.3.0

Relay shares your Mac’s VPN with clients such as smart TVs and PlayStations that cannot run a VPN themselves. Use one VPN connection across multiple clients, or switch them to direct modem internet when you prefer better speed—all without changing their network.

Requires macOS 13 or later.
